DECISION FRAMEWORK (from codify-convention skill and CLAUDE.md
codification routing):

GRADUATE when ALL three hold:
  - Load-bearing: future work materially benefits from naming this
    pattern explicitly. The codified convention would be cited in
    future briefs, ADRs, or other discipline surfaces.
  - Generalizable: the pattern transcends the specific situation that
    surfaced it. The convention file's reach extends beyond the
    immediate originating arc.
  - Stable: the pattern has settled enough that the convention won't
    immediately need amendment. Premature codification of an evolving
    pattern produces stale conventions.

DISMISS when ANY of these hold:
  - Narrow: pattern is specific to a one-time situation, not
    repeatable.
  - Unstable: still evolving, premature to codify, the convention
    would need amendment within weeks.
  - Captured-elsewhere: the same insight already lives in an existing
    convention under a different name; graduating creates a duplicate
    surface.

DEFER when:
  - The pattern meets some but not all graduate criteria.
  - Additional N is needed to demonstrate stability.
  - The codification skill would benefit from one more session of
    operational experience before being applied to this family.
